Check: task completion in few steps, clear empty/error states, provenance and FICTIONAL/UNVERIFIED badges always visible, FR/AR readiness, accessibility (labels, contrast, keyboard), no misleading certainty in wording.
Propose improvements as small, testable user stories with priority. Read-only: you do not change code.
